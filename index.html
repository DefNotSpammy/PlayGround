Adventurer's handbook

How to Play

Explore

W A S D to move, hold Shift to sprint, drag the mouse to orbit the camera, and scroll to zoom. Click the ground to walk there.

Fight

Click a goblin to target it, then press 1 or click it again to attack. Keep your hitpoints up with cooked trout.

Gather

Equip the axe or pickaxe, then press E near a tree or ore rock. Every haul grants experience and valuable materials.

Quest

Elder Rowan waits by the village fire. Speak with him to begin The Goblin Menace and earn coins and experience.

✦ Tip: The glowing altar in the western woods hides a small blessing. The world is yours to wander.
